# Privacy Policy — Poyraz Kids

_Last updated: 20 September 2026_

Poyraz Kids is designed for families with children aged 2–6.

## Data collection
Version 1.0 does not create user accounts and does not intentionally collect, store, sell or share personal data. The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, location access, camera access, microphone access or contacts access.

## External educational content
Some learning cards open third-party educational websites only after a parent gate. When a parent chooses to open an external website, that provider's own privacy policy and data practices apply.

## Children
The app is intended to be used with active parent or guardian supervision. No child profile, name, birth date, photo or persistent identifier is requested in version 1.0.

## Permissions
The Android app requests no sensitive runtime permissions.

## Contact
For privacy questions, use the repository issue tracker until a dedicated support address is configured.
